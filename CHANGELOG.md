# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- First release: the Earth from inside the ISS Cupola as a live wallpaper, on a realistic orbit computed on the device from bundled orbit data that is refreshed once a day from the project's website (optional).
- Day and night, seasonal Earth colours, clouds, atmosphere, city lights, the Moon and stars.
- Look-around by tilting the phone, idle float, two rest views (diagonal, nadir).
- Battery settings: frame rate at rest, quality cap, clouds switch.
- Info screen with the ground point, its local time and the next pass over a home city; share the current view.
- No ads, no tracking. The only network use is the daily download of public ISS orbit data, which can be turned off in the settings.
